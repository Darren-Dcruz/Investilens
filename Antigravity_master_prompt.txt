============================================================
1. PRODUCT VISION
============================================================

InvestiLens is an AI-powered investment research agent.

The product does NOT autonomously buy or sell stocks.

Its purpose is to give a user a transparent research process:

    USER INPUT
        ↓
    RESEARCH PLAN
        ↓
    HUMAN APPROVAL
        ↓
    REAL WEB RESEARCH
        ↓
    STRUCTURED EVIDENCE
        ↓
    CROSS-SOURCE VERIFICATION
        ↓
    FINANCIAL ANALYSIS
        ↓
    NEWS / EVENT / RISK ANALYSIS
        ↓
    BULL + BEAR ANALYSIS
        ↓
    DETERMINISTIC SCORING
        ↓
    HUMAN FINAL APPROVAL
        ↓
    FINAL RESEARCH REPORT

The central product philosophy is:

"Most investment tools give you numbers.
 InvestiLens gives you the research process."

The agent should:

    SEARCH
      ↓
    VERIFY
      ↓
    ANALYZE
      ↓
    CHALLENGE ITS OWN CONCLUSION
      ↓
    ASK THE HUMAN
      ↓
    REPORT

Core differentiators:

1. Browser-native research
2. Multi-source verification
3. Bull + Bear analysis
4. Explainable scoring
5. Human-in-the-loop approval
6. Transparent evidence/confidence
7. No autonomous trading
